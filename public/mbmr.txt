Hello there!
------------

Hi! I am Mathis Randl, a PhD candidate in the SaCS lab at EPFL. I work
on approximate vector search: the algorithms and systems that find
nearest neighbors in large collections of embeddings.

My main projects start from the observation that real-world queries
tend to look like each other. Proximity is a cache that reuses the
results of similar past queries, and CatapultDB uses past queries to
start graph searches closer to their destination.

I also collaborate on searching data that is spread over many owners
(federated and decentralized search), and on measuring what privacy
costs in vector search.

This website is meant to store my CV/resume, as well as archive papers,
blog posts and announcements related to my professional works. Feel free
to explore around!

Leveraging Approximate Caching for Faster Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(Middleware '25)
  S. Bergman, A.-M. Kermarrec, D. Petrescu, R. Pires, M. Randl,
  M. de Vos, J. Zhang
  Designed, programmed and benchmarked Proximity, an LSH-based cache
  that serves vector search queries before they reach the vector DB. It
  reduces the number of calls by more than 75% on workloads with
  real-world locality, with no loss in recall.
  PDF:     https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.05530
  Code:    https://github.com/sacs-epfl/proximity
  arXiv:   https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05530
  ACM:     https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3721462.3770776
